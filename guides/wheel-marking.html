    Before the booth opens

    • Use a sharp pointer tip. A blunt one can cover most of the thin JACKPOT slice.
    • Give the wheel 10 gentle spins. If it keeps stopping at the same spot, that side is heavier. Tape a coin on the opposite side and test again.
    • Every spin must go around at least three full turns.
